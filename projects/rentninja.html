RentNinja AI

A structured tenant-screening workspace that brings applicant review, notes, and workflow status into one place.

RentNinja AI is a Next.js application for operators who need a clearer way to organize applicant information and screening decisions.

The problem

Screening workflows often spread information across forms, notes, files, and follow-up decisions. RentNinja brings those pieces into a multi-tenant workspace with a consistent applicant model and explicit status labels.

What is implemented

The application includes Auth.js authentication, MongoDB/Mongoose persistence, organization-scoped data, applicant CRUD, a 100-point scoring flow, decision labels, red-flag detection, affordability calculation, notes, filtering, sorting, and lease/application status tracking.

AI’s role

AI-assisted workflows include applicant review, applicant comparison, and extracting fields from application files or pasted text. These features sit inside a broader structured workflow so the operator can review and act on the result.

Next step

The next step is to keep tightening the operator experience around configured environments, production integrations, and the handoffs that happen after an applicant is reviewed.
