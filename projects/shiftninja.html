ShiftNinja AI

An application direction for turning scheduling problems into clearer, more manageable workflows.

ShiftNinja AI is part of my exploration of practical automation: taking a repetitive coordination task and giving it a simpler interface, clearer states, and more useful next actions.

The product idea

The intended direction is a scheduling tool that helps people reason about availability, changes, and handoffs without turning the workflow into a spreadsheet exercise.

What I’m exploring

That means making the core state visible, keeping inputs structured, and using AI only where it can help translate messy context into an actionable suggestion.

Current direction

The next step is to turn the scheduling model into a focused workflow: make changes easy to see, reduce coordination overhead, and keep the operator in control of the final plan.
