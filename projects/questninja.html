QuestNinja

A learning application built around practice, feedback, and making progress feel visible.

QuestNinja is the public-facing name for the Russian Math Ninja project. It turns practice into a sequence of approachable challenges that can respond to a learner’s progress.

The problem

Learning tools can become passive when they only present content. QuestNinja explores a more active loop: attempt a problem, receive useful feedback, and keep moving with a clearer sense of what to try next.

The approach

The product centers on short practice interactions and a progress-oriented experience. The idea grew from wanting to make learning practice more engaging and approachable, while keeping the underlying goal clear.

Current direction

The next step is to deepen the practice loop: make feedback more actionable, give learners a better sense of momentum, and keep the interface inviting at every level.
