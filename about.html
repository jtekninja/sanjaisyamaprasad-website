Who I am

Building from the question underneath the feature.

I’m Sanjai Syamaprasad, a Brooklyn-based software engineer and founder of JTekNinja.

My independent work explores how software can make a process easier to understand and act on. QuestNinja grew from an interest in making learning practice more engaging; other projects examine screening workflows, scheduling, and practical automation.

I’m interested in the full path from idea to application: framing the problem, shaping the experience, integrating services, testing behavior, and improving the parts that people actually use.

How I work

Translate. Build. Test. Refine.

I start by making the user, workflow, and constraints concrete. From there I break the problem into a small useful path, choose the simplest architecture that can support it, and test the behavior at the edges.

ChatGPT and Codex are part of my development toolkit for exploring requirements, reviewing implementation ideas, debugging, and iterating. I combine that assistance with hands-on engineering judgment across application code, integrations, data, and testing.
